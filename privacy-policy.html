Privacy Policy for DoodleBuddy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

DoodleBuddy does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Drawings, character images, selected background photos, and saved characters are stored locally on the user's device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

In-app purchases for character save slots are processed by Google Play. DoodleBuddy does not receive or store payment card information.

Users may delete saved characters inside the app. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data.
